Scrap Management
Scrap is auto-generated from Wear & Tear part replacements — no manual counting. Log a vendor sale when a pickup happens.
How to read the numbers below: All-time figures (Est. Generated, Backlog) never reset and ignore the "From" date — they answer "how much is outstanding right now?" This range figures (Sold, Revenue) respect the date filter above — they answer "what happened in this window?" Discrepancy is a different thing entirely — it's how far one single movement was from its own expected quantity, not a category-wide total.
