RMC Job Card Upload

Upload the monthly PAMSale-style Excel export for RMC cost data
Expects columns: Location, Billing No., Job No, Billing Date, Bill Type, Book Name, Reg Number, City, Model Name, Part ID/Labor Category ID, Part Name/Labor Cateogry Name, Category, Qty, Amount, Issue Catg, Kms.Run, Draft JC, Manual JC remarks, Lab Time, Mechanic, Part Return catg, Bike status, Job Card Opened Date, Job Card Billed Date, Hub Name, Pricing Type, User ID, Submission Method, Reason for Ending, Submission Type, RFD Created At, RFD Status, Technician Name, DMS JC ID, Executive Name, TAT (Days), JC Detail, Production Batch, Vehicle Sub Status, Recovery TAT (Days), Recovery TAT Bucket, User Age Bucket, Non Charged Damage Items, Avg Daily KM (Last 30 Days Before JC), Avg Daily KM Bucket.
Re-uploading a job card (matched by Location + Job No + Reg Number) replaces all its line items with what's in the new file — no leftover/stale lines from before, and job cards not present in the new file are left untouched.
