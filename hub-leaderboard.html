How the score works

Each hub gets a score from 0 to 100 on every measure, based on its position among the ranked hubs (100 = best hub, 0 = worst). Scores are relative, so they show who is ahead and who is behind, not whether a target was met.

  • Operations (70% of the overall score): OOS % of fleet · aged OOS bikes (over 7 days) per 100 bikes · repairs completed within 48 hours · net OOS clearing per 100 bikes (OOS repairs finished minus new rider, exchange and recovery inflow) · labour hours per technician per day (logged hours, real technicians with 3+ working days, FREELANCER excluded).
  • Control & quality (30%): MSL parts short · customer rating (needs 20+ ratings) · damage fee collected (needs 10+ damage job cards) · latest audit score.
  • Missing data is never counted as zero. A hub is ranked only if it has at least 4 of the 5 operations measures, and gets a control & quality score only if it has at least 2 of the 4. Otherwise it is ranked on operations alone.
  • Tiers are thirds of the ranking: Good (top third), Watch, Needs attention (bottom third). They are relative, so some hubs are always in the bottom third.
  • Fleet measures (OOS %, aged OOS) are as of now. The others cover the dates selected above.
  • Comparison set: the main service hubs only. The plant, pilot, test and very small sites are left out, and partner hubs (for example "Flipkart hub - Hoodi") count under their parent hub.
