Forecast vs Actual

—

Hub Replacement Check

Actual vs what each hub's own bikes were expected to need
Expected = what the forecast says this hub's bikes needed (from km run and part age), adjusted to the city's overall level so a city-wide change does not flag every hub. Over = at least 1.3× expected, Under = at most 0.77× expected, and only when the gap is too big to be chance (2+ standard deviations). Hubs with fewer than 10 expected are left out. Replaced / Came, not replaced / Didn't come = what happened to this hub's forecast bucket (its most likely bikes, as many as its forecast).
